# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in tpt-identity to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues and discussions
- Giving and accepting feedback on reports with grace
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use this software
- Showing empathy towards other participants

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without their permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, PhillipC05, through a GitHub issue marked as a conduct report, or privately through the [GitHub security advisory form](https://github.com/tpt-solutions/tpt-identity/security/advisories/new) if the matter should not be public. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behaviour is serious or repeated.
